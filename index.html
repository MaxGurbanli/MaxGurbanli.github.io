003

Evaluating Heuristic Explanations Against Formal Sufficiency

B.Sc. Thesis · Maastricht University

Tested LIME, SHAP and Anchors against logic-based sufficient reasons, computed with SAT-based enumeration, on the Adult Income and German Credit datasets. SHAP reached 99% sufficiency on Adult Income with seven features, while LIME was slower and Anchors stayed near 0%. On the higher-dimensional German Credit, sufficiency stayed low and making the explanations sufficient took many extra features.

Explainable AI SAT Python
